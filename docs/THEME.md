# Two Channels

A custom theme in the theme-factory format, made for a scientist who works on both sides of the bench. It borrows from two-color fluorescence microscopy: a soft, light field, one channel for research, one for code, and a third color only where they overlap.

## Color Palette

- **Mist**: `#F3F5F4` — page background, soft and light
- **Well**: `#E8EDEB` — quiet panels, code blocks
- **Plate**: `#FAFBFA` — figure plates
- **Fixative Ink**: `#1C2830` — text and primary buttons
- **Research Cyan**: `#0E6E80` — research and wet-lab work, links, focus
- **Code Magenta**: `#A8336E` — software and dry-lab work
- **Merge Violet**: `#5A3F8F` — work that is both

Light-only by design. All text colors meet WCAG AA contrast on the mist background.

## Typography

- **Headers and interface**: Schibsted Grotesk (600–800, tight tracking for display sizes)
- **Reading text**: Source Serif 4 (bios, summaries, articles; 1.6–1.72 line height)
- **Code only**: JetBrains Mono (ligatures off)

## Principles

- One bold element per page. On the home page that's Fig. 0, a career drawn as a single-cell embedding; everything around it stays quiet.
- Color carries meaning. Cyan, magenta, and violet always mean research, code, and both.
- Figures are plates. Every graphic sits on the same pale plate with a hairline border, like a figure panel.
- Motion only once. The Fig. 0 points condense into clusters on load; nothing else animates unless you act on it. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Best Used For

Research-developer portfolios, lab pages, computational biology and biotech personal sites.
