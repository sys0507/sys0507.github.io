Miao Li

Scientist working at the interface of cell therapy, computational biology, and AI-enabled drug discovery.

I bridge experimental and computational discovery. I build AI-powered platforms, deep learning models, and multi-omics and immune-repertoire pipelines that turn single-cell and NGS data into translational insight for TCR and cell therapy discovery, and I test what they predict with my own hands at the bench.

Wet lab
Immunology, CAR-T and TCR-T cell engineering, stem cell-derived T cells, antigen-specific TCR screening
Dry lab
NGS and single-cell bioinformatics, AI/ML modeling of immune repertoires, building AI agents

What excites me most right now: AI agents that take on real problems in biomedicine and drug discovery.
